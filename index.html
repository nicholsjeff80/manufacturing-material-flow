Five-minute walkthrough

1. Acknowledge REQ-101, requesting 60 units.

2. Record DEMO-BOX-A with 40 units. The request stays open with a 20-unit shortage.

3. Submit that same serial again. It counts once.

4. Record DEMO-BOX-B with 20 units. The request completes.

5. Enter a correction reason and void BOX-B. The shortage reopens and the acknowledgment time stays the same. Restore the delivery to complete it again.

Recovery & exception review

For another acknowledged request, choose “Summary update fails” or “Response lost after save.” The delivery appears in evidence while the queue is marked stale. Reconcile or retry the same serial to repair the summary without counting twice.

Try a serial already used by a different request, disable the simulated connection, or close a shortage with a reason. Closure retains physical delivery evidence.
